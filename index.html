Work

Speech recognition at BharatGen, from data to serving

Generative AI Engineer at BharatGen, IIT Bombay, since May 2025. Promoted from AI Software Engineer in November 2025.

Released models
Contributed to Shrutam-2, BharatGen’s LLM-based speech recognizer. Co-trained Shrutam-HindiASR-1.0, an E-Branchformer model at 11.58 WER on Vistaar Hindi (13.6 for IndicWhisper), and wrote the ESPnet branch it runs inference on.
Data and training
Curated and quality-filtered 40,000+ hours of speech with WER-based scoring, and ran multi-GPU, multi-node training.
Serving
Wrote inference servers for clients, with Silero VAD and batched inference.
Evaluation
Built WER and CER evaluation pipelines across Whisper, Conformer and Branchformer models.
Research
LLM-based correction of ASR output, and code-switched ASR. Both are papers under review.
