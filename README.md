Third-party material keeps its own license:

- `fonts/bricolage-grotesque.woff2`: Bricolage Grotesque, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`fonts/OFL.txt`).
- `audio/rice.flac`: the passage from "We choose to go to the Moon in this decade" to "and the others, too" in President John F. Kennedy's address at Rice University, Houston, 12 September 1962. The recording was made by the White House Communications Agency and is in the public domain (JFK Library, JFKWHA-127-002); this copy is from Wikimedia Commons (`File:Jfk_rice_university_we_choose_to_go_to_the_moon.ogg`).
- `audio/rice.json`: word and phone timings for that passage (phones in IPA), from a forced alignment with CMU PocketSphinx (BSD license) and its US English model.
- The theme palettes in `js/palette.js` are reduced to two colours from the community themes in [Monkeytype](https://github.com/monkeytypegame/monkeytype) (GPL-3.0); credit for the original palettes goes to their authors there.
